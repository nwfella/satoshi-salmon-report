**Investment Thesis**: All three risk analysts converge on the same action — cut BTC-USD exposure — and the only real dispute is magnitude and whether to short. That convergence, plus the most recent lesson (the 2026-09-05 Hold at ~$79.8K was effectively wrong: -1.7% raw / -1.5% alpha because a negative MACD histogram and sub-1:1 reward/risk into a binary FOMC were treated as a ban on buying rather than a trigger to trim), tells me the correct posture is a partial reduction, not a passive hold and not a full exit. Underweight expresses exactly that: reduce net exposure below benchmark, keep the residual long, do not turn tactical weakness into a directional short.

The bear/aggressive side wins the near-term tactical debate on hard data. BTC-USD is failing below every short-term reference: the 10 EMA (77,962), the 20 SMA (78,587), and the VWMA (78,602.70), which is why the trader's 78,600 pivot is the bulls' line in the sand. Macro is a live headwind, not priced relief: 10Y at 4.95%, 2Y at 4.56%, DXY firming toward 100, a bear-flattening curve, and a hawkish Sept 16 FOMC where prediction markets flipped hard — the $82,500 contract collapsed 41 points to 34% while the $75,000 dip probability surged 27 points to 76%. High real rates are a direct tax on a zero-yield, no-earnings, no-balance-sheet asset, and the fundamentals report's HOLD reflects missing data, not a bullish floor. Critically, the aggressive analyst's squeeze catalyst is upside down: StockTwits at 82% bullish means retail is crowded LONG, which creates flush risk (those holders become sellers if 76,118 breaks), not fuel. That symmetry of positioning also caps the aggressive case: with retail already long, a squeeze higher requires trapped shorts, and there is no evidence of that.

But the bull and the neutral analyst correctly block a full Sell or a net short. The medium-term trend is still intact: the golden cross fired ~Sept 8 (50 SMA 70,908.95 over 200 SMA 70,091.13), price sits ~9% above the 50 SMA and ~10% above the 200 SMA, higher lows are in place, RSI reset to 55.10 without decisively breaking 50, M2 is expanding at 5.09% YoY, recession odds are only ~10%, and there is a hard support shelf at 76,118–76,162. Bollinger squeezes resolve in the direction of the prevailing trend about as often as not, so a squeeze higher is a real scenario — and the aggressive analyst's own 82,500 stop is only ~5% above the 78,600 pivot, less than two ATRs, meaning a routine reclaim to 81,056/81,271 would stop out a short before the downside ever pays. That is a coin flip with crypto slippage, not asymmetric risk. The 2026-09-02 lesson reinforces this: fading momentum above a rising 200 SMA cost +3.1% raw / +2.4% alpha. Shorting a confirmed uptrend over a tactical macro read would repeat that mistake.

The synthesis is therefore Underweight with staged, level-based execution rather than a single-event SELL. Start reducing now toward 65–75% of benchmark — not conditional on 78,600 filling — because price is already at ~77,270 below the pivot cluster with the FOMC close. Use 78,600–80,000 as an additional trim zone (where the bear expects supply and the bull's own breakout trigger sits — a failed reclaim is the cue to lighten further). Cut toward ~50% or lower if 76,118 breaks on rising volume, with the 50 SMA at 70,908.95 (-8% from spot) and ~65,000 as the next magnets. Manage downside with defined-risk puts sized so premium doesn't eat the portfolio — never a naked short. Re-risk only on stabilization: a daily close reclaiming 78,600 (stop trimming, consider adding back) with improving internals (MACD histogram stops widening, price reclaims the VWMA), or a defended 75,000 retest on drying volume; upgrade toward neutral/Overweight only on a sustained reclaim of 82,500 with Fed-hike odds below 50% and VIX back under 15. This captures the right tail (breakout continuation) while capping the far more probable near-term downside through a hawkish FOMC.
